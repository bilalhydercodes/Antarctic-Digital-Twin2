THE PROBLEM SPACE

Mission-Critical Constraints: Why Antarctica Needs a Digital Twin

Operating in East Antarctica presents engineering hazards with zero margin for error. A single component oversight can endanger lives and mission continuity.

❄️
Extreme Sub-Zero Thermal Shock
Temperatures plunge below -50°C with 200+ km/h katabatic blizzards. In these temperatures, fresh water in delivery pipelines can freeze and rupture pipes within 45 minutes if electric trace heating fails.
Priyadarshini Loop Criticality: > +2.0°C
🚢
6-Month Polar Isolation
During Antarctic winter, access by ship or plane is physically impossible. Stations must maintain absolute autonomy over AN-8 fuel reserves, freeze-dried food, water generation, and power microgrids.
47 Winter Crew • Zero External Support
🛰️
Satellite Bottlenecks & Outages
Geomagnetic storms, polar cap absorption (PCA), and low orbital satellite elevation angles create recurring blackout windows. Systems must function offline with store-and-forward edge sync.
Simulated Ka-band & VSAT degradation
The Digital Twin Solution:
A real-time cyber-physical bridge that detects thermal anomalies before freezing occurs, models generator wear hours, visualizes structural interiors, and calculates runway days for fuel, food, and water.

COMMUNICATIONS RESILIENCE

Edge Gateway: Satellite Bandwidth Throttling & Offline Buffering

Designed from the ground up for high-latitude polar conditions where satellite connectivity is intermittent, slow, and expensive.

🗜️
92% Telemetry Compression
Raw JSON sensor packets are serialized, deduplicated, and compressed into binary delta payloads before uplink, reducing costly satellite bandwidth overhead.
Bandwidth Saved: ~92.4%
📦
Store-and-Forward Queue
When satellite links disconnect during blizzards or low satellite passes, the local Edge Gateway buffers all sensor telemetry safely in an offline queue.
Zero Data Loss During Outages
🔄
Automated Batch Re-Sync
As soon as the satellite link re-establishes, the gateway flushes queued packets in ordered batches to central NCPOR headquarters in Goa via /api/telemetry/sync.
Intelligent Burst Transmission
📡 Satellite Simulation Banner: The top banner allows judges to toggle connection status between LOCAL BROADBAND, SATELLITE (DEGRADED), and OFFLINE to witness real-time queueing and recovery.
